# 完整示例：从一篇英文文章到一次对照翻译

> 示例文章为**虚构素材**，只为演示排版密度与翻译口径，不要当作真实报道引用。
> 按示例逐段输出英文引用块和对应中文翻译，标题也做对照，最后一段译完即止。

---

## 输入（用户发来的内容）

```
The Four-Day Week Gets a Second Look

A decade ago, the four-day week was widely dismissed as a perk for start-ups with too much cash and too little discipline. The tone has since shifted. A string of pilots run by the Workweek Institute, a Manchester-based research group, suggests that trimming a day does not, as critics once warned, hollow out output.

The results are hardly uniform. In some pilots, teams reported that meetings were cut and the pace of work tightened; in others, managers quietly complained that client-facing staff were left holding the bag on Fridays. Much depends, the Institute's director Okafor argues, on whether a firm treats the change as a redesign of how work gets done or as a cosmetic perk tacked onto an unchanged workload.

Sceptics remain. A partner at a mid-sized consultancy calls the pilots self-flattering: the firms that volunteer are already lean, and the staff who opt in are the ones with the most slack. Whether the model scales to a warehouse floor or a hospital ward is a question the pilot literature has largely ducked. Even Okafor concedes that the four-day week remains, for most employers, a hard sell.
```

## 输出

> The Four-Day Week Gets a Second Look

四天工作制再次受到关注

> A decade ago, the four-day week was widely dismissed as a perk for start-ups with too much cash and too little discipline. The tone has since shifted. A string of pilots run by the Workweek Institute, a Manchester-based research group, suggests that trimming a day does not, as critics once warned, hollow out output.

十年前，四天工作制普遍被当成一种福利——只有钱太多、规矩太少的初创公司才玩得起。如今口风变了。曼彻斯特一家研究机构"工作周研究所"开展的一连串试点显示，每周少上一天，并不会像批评者当年警告的那样把产出掏空。

> The results are hardly uniform. In some pilots, teams reported that meetings were cut and the pace of work tightened; in others, managers quietly complained that client-facing staff were left holding the bag on Fridays. Much depends, the Institute's director Okafor argues, on whether a firm treats the change as a redesign of how work gets done or as a cosmetic perk tacked onto an unchanged workload.

结果并不整齐。有些试点里，团队反馈会议减少了、工作节奏反而更紧；另一些试点里，经理们私下抱怨，周五的活全压在对接客户的人身上。所长奥卡福认为，关键取决于公司把这件事当成什么：是对工作方式的一次重新设计，还是在工作量一点没减的情况下，额外挂上去的一个好看福利。

> Sceptics remain. A partner at a mid-sized consultancy calls the pilots self-flattering: the firms that volunteer are already lean, and the staff who opt in are the ones with the most slack. Whether the model scales to a warehouse floor or a hospital ward is a question the pilot literature has largely ducked. Even Okafor concedes that the four-day week remains, for most employers, a hard sell.

质疑的声音也还在。一家中型咨询公司的合伙人说，这些试点是在自我美化：愿意参加的公司本来就人手精简，愿意报名的员工也本来就是手上余量最多的那些人。这套模式能不能放进仓库车间、能不能放进医院病区，是试点研究基本绕开了的问题。就连奥卡福也承认，对大多数雇主来说，四天工作制依然很难推得动。

## 两处对照说明（只在示例里讲，实际输出不写）

- `left holding the bag`、`a hard sell`、`hollow out` 这类习语**不单列条目**，直接靠译文顺出来：译成"活全压在……身上""很难推得动""把产出掏空"，读中文就知道意思，不需要解释字面。
- 译名在阅读全文时统一（the Workweek Institute → 工作周研究所，Okafor → 奥卡福），后文一律沿用，不在每段重新交代。
